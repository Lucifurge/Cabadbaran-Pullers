ABOUT CABADBARAN PULLERS

Building champions. Building friendships. Building a stronger arm wrestling community.

Our Story

Cabadbaran Pullers was founded to unite athletes who share a passion for arm wrestling and to promote the sport in Cabadbaran City and throughout Agusan del Norte. Our goal is to create a welcoming environment where beginners and experienced competitors alike can train, learn, and compete while developing strength, discipline, sportsmanship, and lasting friendships.

Through regular training sessions, tournaments, and community events, Cabadbaran Pullers aims to represent our city with pride while inspiring the next generation of pullers.

OUR PURPOSE

MISSION & VISION

MISSION

To develop competitive arm wrestlers by promoting discipline, integrity, teamwork, respect and sportsmanship while building a strong and united community that welcomes everyone who wishes to learn and compete.

VISION

To become one of the leading arm wrestling clubs in Mindanao and produce athletes capable of competing in regional, national and international events while proudly representing Cabadbaran City.

WHY JOIN US

WHY ARM WRESTLING?

Increase Strength

Build stronger hands, wrists, forearms, biceps, shoulders, back and overall pulling power.

Competitive Spirit

Compete in tournaments, improve your skills and challenge yourself against other athletes.

Brotherhood

Meet new friends, train together and become part of an ever-growing family.

Represent Cabadbaran

Represent our city in local, regional, and national competitions.
