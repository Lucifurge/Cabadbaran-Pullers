WHO WE ARE

ABOUT CABADBARAN PULLERS

More than an arm wrestling club. We are a growing family built on discipline, respect, sportsmanship, and brotherhood.

Strength Begins With Community

Cabadbaran Pullers was founded to unite athletes who share one passion—arm wrestling. Our mission is to provide a welcoming environment where beginners and experienced competitors can train together, improve their skills, and represent Cabadbaran City with pride.

Through weekly training sessions, local tournaments, outreach events, and athlete development, we continue building one of the fastest-growing arm wrestling communities in Mindanao.

Strong Community

Everyone is welcome regardless of experience.

Professional Training

Learn proper technique, safety, and competition skills.

OUR MISSION

To develop disciplined, respectful, and competitive arm wrestlers while creating a welcoming community where athletes can grow, compete, and inspire others.

OUR VISION

To become one of the leading arm wrestling organizations in Mindanao and proudly represent Cabadbaran City in regional, national, and international competitions.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

HAVE QUESTIONS?

Do I need experience?

No. Beginners are always welcome. We will teach you proper arm wrestling techniques from the ground up.

Where do you train?

Training is held at Peak Gym, Cabadbaran City.

Can I pay onsite?

Yes. On-site payment is accepted during training sessions and official tournaments.

How long before payment is verified?

Usually within 24 hours after sending your payment receipt.

Can anyone join?

Yes. Whether you're a beginner, fitness enthusiast, or competitive athlete, everyone is welcome.
