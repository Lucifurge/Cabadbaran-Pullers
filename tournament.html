Tournament Overview

The Cabadbaran Pullers Opening Rankings Tournament is an official ranking event that establishes the competitive standing of athletes under the Cabadbaran Pullers organization.

Athletes will compete through strength, technique, discipline, and sportsmanship to secure their place in the official Top 10 rankings.

This event begins the official Cabadbaran Pullers ranking system and will serve as the foundation for future tournaments.

Official Rankings Battle

The first official Cabadbaran Pullers rankings already have established positions. This tournament focuses on athletes competing for the remaining Top 10 positions.
