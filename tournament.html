Tournament Overview

The Cabadbaran Pullers Super Tournament will bring together armwrestlers for a major competitive event celebrating strength, discipline, sportsmanship, and the growth of arm wrestling in Cabadbaran City and beyond.

The tournament will feature a dedicated Newbie — Open Weight division exclusively for Cabadbaran newcomers, together with open categories for women's, light, middle, and heavyweight competitors.

Our goal is to build a strong, competitive, and welcoming arm wrestling community while giving athletes the opportunity to test themselves on the table.
