Tournament Overview

The Cabadbaran Pullers Opening Rankings Tournament marks the beginning of the official Cabadbaran Pullers ranking system.

Athletes will compete to establish their position in the first official rankings of Cabadbaran Pullers.

This tournament will recognize strength, technique, discipline, and sportsmanship among arm wrestling athletes in Cabadbaran.

Ranking System
