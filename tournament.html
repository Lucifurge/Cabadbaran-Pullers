Tournament Overview

The Cabadbaran Pullers Opening Rankings Tournament marks the beginning of the official Cabadbaran Pullers ranking system.

Athletes will compete to establish their position, prove their strength, technique, discipline, and sportsmanship in the Cabadbaran armwrestling community.

This tournament will determine the next official ranked pullers of Cabadbaran Pullers.

Ranking Category

The official Cabadbaran Pullers rankings have already established the current Top 1 to Top 4 positions.

This tournament is a battle for the remaining ranking positions from Rank #5 to Rank #10.
